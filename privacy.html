Who we are

What this website collects

Analytics, only if you accept. Google Analytics records the pages you visit, your approximate city, your device and browser, how you arrived, and which buttons you tap to Shopee, TikTok Shop, WhatsApp or Instagram. IP addresses are anonymised. We use this only to improve the site, never for advertising.

Nothing else is stored by us. There are no accounts and no forms that send data to our servers. The horse and stable enquiry form only prepares a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

Cookies

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies whose names start with _ga. They stay in your browser for up to two years unless you clear them or decline. Google keeps the analytics data for no longer than 14 months. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set, and any existing ones are deleted.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like any web host, these services receive your IP address when the page loads. Messages you send us on WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta). Orders placed on Shopee or TikTok Shop are covered by those platforms' own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022), you may ask what personal data we hold about you, correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Message us on WhatsApp and we will respond.
