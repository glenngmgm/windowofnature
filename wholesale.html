Before we begin

A few useful details.

Can I enquire about alfalfa for horses?

Yes. Share the size of your operation, destination and planned requirements. We can discuss the current alfalfa range and the product information your nutrition team needs.

Which pack sizes are available?

Our core alfalfa range is 500 g, 1 kg and 11 kg. For a business order, enquire about quantities, current availability and delivery to your location.

Do you publish wholesale prices?

We discuss pricing directly, based on the product and your order requirements. Send an enquiry to start the conversation.

Can I speak with someone before ordering?

Yes. All enquiries and orders go to WON on WhatsApp at +62 813-9177-9997.
