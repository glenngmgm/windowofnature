Our mill

Provenance,
from field to feed

WON stands for Window of Nature. At our mill in Bandung, West Java, we oversee every stage of production: sourcing Italian and Australian alfalfa, milling, pressing and packing. Nothing is outsourced, and nothing is preserved with chemicals.

It is how every batch stays consistent and traceable, whether it is destined for a single companion or a farm of a thousand.
